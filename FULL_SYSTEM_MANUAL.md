## 1. Platform Overview

**PRIME Connect** is the unified operating system for Prime Hotels Group. It connects every employee, department, and property into a single digital workplace.

### Key Concepts
*   **Role-Based Access**: What you see depends on who you are. A Housekeeper sees different options than a General Manager.
*   **Bilingual**: Every screen works in English and Arabic.
*   **Mobile-First**: Designed to work on your phone as well as your computer.

---

## 2. Getting Started: First Steps

### Logging In
1.  Navigate to the portal URL.
2.  Enter your **Official Hotel Email** (e.g., `name@primehotels.sa`).
3.  Enter your secure password.
4.  *(First Time Only)* You may be asked to change your password.

### Setting Up Your Profile
1.  Click your **Avatar** (Profile Picture) in the bottom-left sidebar.
2.  Select **"My Profile"**.
3.  **Personal Info**: Check your details. Contact HR if your Job Title is wrong.
4.  **Documents**: Upload your ID, Passport, or Certificates in the "Documents" tab.
    *   *System Logic*: The system will alert you 30 days before a document expires.

### Customizing Preferences
1.  Go to **Settings** (via Profile Menu).
2.  **Notifications**: Choose if you want Emails, Push Notifications, or both.
3.  **Language**: Toggle between English/Arabic.
4.  **Security**: Change your password or sign out of other devices.

## 3. The Dashboard: Your Command Center

Your Dashboard is personalized based on your role.

### For Everyone (Staff Dashboard)
*   **Quick Stats**: Pending Tasks, Leave Balance, Next Shift.
*   **Announcements**: Critical updates from HQ.
*   **My Schedule**: Your roster for the week.

### For Managers (Manager Dashboard)
*   **Team Overview**: Who is on duty? Who is absent?
*   **Approvals**: Pending requests requiring your action.
*   **Operational KPIs**: Occupancy, Guest Satisfaction (from PMS).

## 4. Module A: My HR Self-Service

This module empowers you to manage your own employment needs.

### Requesting Leave
1.  Click **"My Requests"** -> **"+ New Request"**.
2.  Select **"Leave Request"**.
3.  **Dates**: Choose logic-checked dates (e.g., you cannot pick a past date).
4.  **Reason**: Add notes.
5.  **Submit**.
    *   *Routing*: Goes to Supervisor -> HR -> Approved.

### Checking Attendance
1.  Click **"My Attendance"**.
2.  View your punch-in/out times.
3.  **Dispute**: If a record is wrong (e.g., "Forgot to punch out"), click **"Request Correction"**. Your manager must approve this.

### Performance & Goals
1.  **"My Goals"**: See objectives assigned by your manager. Update your progress (0-100%).
2.  **"Performance"**: View your annual/quarterly reviews and scores.

### Downloading Payslips
1.  Click **"My Payslips"**.
2.  Select the Month/Year.
3.  Click **Download PDF**.
    *   *Security*: This file is password-protected (usually your ID number).

## 5. Module B: Operations & Maintenance

### Reporting Issues (Maintenance)
1.  Click **"Maintenance"** -> **"Submit Ticket"**.
2.  **Location**: Select Property -> Floor -> Room.
3.  **Photo**: Upload an image (Mobile camera supported).
4.  **Voice Memo**: Record a brief description (optional).
5.  **Submit**.
    *   *AI Feature*: The system auto-assigns priority based on keywords (e.g., "Leak" = High).

### Approving Tasks (Managers)
1.  Go to **"Approvals"** (Inbox).
2.  Review details.
3.  **Action**:
    *   **Approve**: Validated.
    *   **Reject**: Denied (Must provide reason).
    *   **Return**: Send back to employee for correction.

### Delegating Authority
Going on leave? Don't block operations.
1.  Go to **Requests Inbox** -> **"Delegations"**.
2.  **Add Delegation**: Select a colleague.
3.  **Duration**: Set Start/End dates.
4.  *Result*: They get your approval rights temporarily. Audit logs track this.

## 6. Module C: Knowledge & Training

### Reading SOPs (Knowledge Base)
1.  Click **"Knowledge Base"**.
2.  Browse Categories (Front Office, Housekeeping, Safety).
3.  **Search**: Use the global search bar.
4.  **Read & Acknowledge**: Click "I have read this" if required by compliance.

### Taking Courses & Quizzes
1.  Click **"My Training"**.
2.  **"Start"**: Launch a course.
3.  **Content**: Watch video / Read slides.
4.  **Quiz**: Pass the test (e.g., 80% passing score).
5.  **Certificate**: Auto-generated PDF upon completion.

## 8. Module E: HR Management (For HR Only)

### Managing Staff (Directory & User Roles)
1.  **"Directory"**: Search any employee.
2.  **"Edit Profile"**: (HR Only) Update Job Title, Department, Salary Info.
3.  **"Roles"**: Assign system permissions (e.g., "Property Manager").

### Promotions & Transfers
1.  Go to **"Promotions"** or **"Transfers"**.
2.  **Initiate**: Select Employee -> New Role/Property -> Logic Check (Budget etc.).
3.  **Workflow**: Requires multi-level approval (HR -> GM -> Regional).

### Shift Scheduling
1.  **"Scheduling"**: View calendar.
2.  **"Add Shift"**: Pick Employee + Time.
3.  **"Publish"**: Notify staff of their roster.

### Onboarding Tracker
1.  **"Onboarding"**: View new hires.
2.  **Checklist**: Track progress (Signed contract? ID received? Uniform issued?).

## 9. Module F: System Administration (Admin Only)

> **Note**: For deep technical details, routing algorithms, and troubleshooting, please refer to the **[Administrator & Technical Guide](file:///c:/Users/mahro/Desktop/prime-hotels%20-%20Copy%20-%20Copy/ADMIN_TECHNICAL_GUIDE.md)**.

### Routing Logic & Health
*   **Logic**: The system auto-routes requests based on the Org Chart (Supervisor -> HR -> Admin).
*   **Health Dashboard**: Shows "Broken" routes (e.g., Manager left company) and allows manual reassignment.
*   **Fix**: Reassign reporting lines immediately in the User Directory.

### Notification Settings
*   **Global Broadcasts**: Send "System Down" or "Emergency" alerts.
*   **Templates**: Edit email content for automated messages.
*   **Batches**: Monitor the background jobs sending mass emails.

### Security & Audit
*   **"Audit Logs"**: See EVERY action taken in the system (Who clicked what, when).
*   **"PII Access"**: Specifically tracks who viewed sensitive data (Passport numbers, Salaries).
*   **AI Tools**: Configure the Sentiment Analysis and Onboarding Path generators.
