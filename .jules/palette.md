## 2025-05-15 - [Search Bar Keyboard Shortcut Hint]
**Learning:** Adding a platform-aware keyboard shortcut hint (⌘K / Ctrl+K) to the global search bar significantly improves discoverability of navigation features. It's a small touch that bridge the gap between casual mouse users and power users.
**Action:** Always consider adding visual shortcut hints to primary action inputs, ensuring they are platform-aware and don't clutter the UI during active interaction (hide on focus/query).
