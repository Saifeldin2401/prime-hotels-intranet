# Altus Connect - Employee Portal

## Your All-in-One Workplace Hub

**Altus Connect** is a comprehensive employee portal designed specifically for Altus Advisory Group. It brings together everything employees need to stay connected, informed, and productive—all in one place.

---

## 🏨 What Can You Do?

### For All Employees

**Stay Informed**
- View important company announcements and updates
- Access the employee directory to find and contact colleagues
- Check your personal dashboard for tasks, shifts, and notifications

**Manage Your Work**
- View and manage your assigned tasks
- Check your work schedule and shifts
- Track your training progress and complete required courses

**HR Self-Service**
- Submit time-off requests and track their status
- Access your personal documents (contracts, payslips, certificates)
- Update your profile information
- Refer candidates for open positions and earn bonuses

**Communication**
- Send and receive messages with colleagues
- Participate in team conversations
- Stay updated with the social feed

---

### For Managers & Department Heads

**Team Oversight**
- View your team's schedules and availability
- Approve or reject leave requests
- Assign and track tasks for your team
- Monitor training completion rates

**Approvals**
- Review and approve expense claims
- Process promotion requests
- Delegate approvals when you're away

---

### For HR Professionals

**Employee Management**
- Onboard new employees
- Manage employee records and documents
- Track compliance with required training
- Process leave requests across departments

**Recruitment**
- Post and manage job openings
- Review applications
- Track employee referrals

**Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)**
- Create and publish company procedures
- Track employee acknowledgments
- Manage version history

---

### For Administrators

**Complete Control**
- Manage user accounts and access levels
- Configure properties and departments
- View audit logs for security
- Activate or deactivate user accounts

---

## 🌍 Built for Everyone

**Bilingual Support**
- Full English and Arabic interface
- Automatic right-to-left text for Arabic users

**Works Everywhere**
- Desktop computers
- Tablets
- Mobile phones

**Secure**
- Password-protected accounts
- Automatic logout after inactivity
- Role-based access (you only see what you need)
