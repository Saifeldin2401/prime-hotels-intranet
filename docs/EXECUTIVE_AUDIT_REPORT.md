# PHG CONNECT - EXECUTIVE AUDIT REPORT
## Comprehensive System Assessment for Multi-Property Hospitality Operations

**Date:** February 2026  
**Scope:** Full operational, financial, HR, commercial, and executive intelligence capabilities  
**Assessment Framework:** Governance, Scalability, Automation, Executive Oversight  

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Overall System Maturity: **65/100** (Partially Digitized Platform)

PHG Connect demonstrates **strong foundational architecture** with enterprise-grade technology stack, comprehensive database design (120+ tables), and sophisticated role-based access control. However, critical gaps in financial governance, commercial revenue management, and executive intelligence prevent it from functioning as a true **centralized command and control platform**.

### Key Findings:
| Aspect | Status | Maturity |
|--------|--------|----------|
| **Core Architecture** | ✅ Production-Ready | High |
| **HR & Workforce** | ✅ Comprehensive | High |
| **Operational Control** | ⚠️ Functional but Gaps | Medium |
| **Financial Governance** | ❌ Major Gaps | Low |
| **Commercial & Revenue** | ❌ Incomplete | Low |
| **Executive Intelligence** | ⚠️ Basic Dashboards | Medium |
| **Cross-Property Control** | ⚠️ Limited | Medium |

### Critical Risk Assessment: **MEDIUM-HIGH**

The system risks becoming an **expensive digital filing cabinet** rather than an operational command center. Without immediate investment in financial controls, commercial modules, and executive analytics, the platform cannot support true multi-property hospitality group operations.
