## 1. Getting Started

1. Open the app and sign in with your email and password.
2. If you forgot your password, use the "Forgot Password" link.
3. On first login, you may be asked to complete onboarding or update your profile.
4. You will land on a dashboard tailored to your role.

## 2. Navigation Basics

- The left sidebar is your main navigation.
- The top bar shows your profile, notifications, and global actions.
- If you see a lock or are redirected, your role does not have access.
- Use breadcrumbs and "Back" actions to return to previous pages.

## 5. Training and Learning

Training features are split into two experiences:

- Admin experience for building and assigning training.
- Learner experience for completing assignments.

### 5.1 Training Hub (Admin)

Use Training Hub to manage modules, build courses, and assign learning.

Key sections inside Training Hub:

- List: View all training modules.
- Builder: Create or edit modules.
- Assignments: Assign modules to people.
- Analytics: Review performance and completion.

### 5.2 Create a Training Module

1. Go to Training Hub.
2. Select "Create Module" or open an existing module.
3. Provide the title, description, and metadata.
4. Build the module with content blocks such as Text, Video, Quiz, SOP Reference, Document Link, Image, Audio, or Interactive.
5. Save drafts anytime.
6. Preview and publish when ready.

### 5.3 Assign Training

1. Open Training Hub and go to Assignments.
2. Select a module.
3. Choose the target audience.
4. Options include Everyone, Users, Departments, Properties, or Roles.
5. Set due dates and priority if needed.
6. Confirm assignment.

### 5.4 Training Rules

Some roles can manage assignment rules:

1. Go to Training Assignment Rules.
2. Define automatic rules by role, department, or property.
3. Save rules to auto-assign training for new or updated users.

### 5.5 Training Paths

Training Paths group modules into a structured sequence.

1. Go to Training Paths.
2. Create a path and add modules in order.
3. Publish and assign the path like any other training.

### 5.6 Quizzes

- Quiz Builder creates assessments.
- Quiz Player allows learners to complete quizzes.

Steps to create a quiz:

1. Go to Quizzes.
2. Click "New Quiz".
3. Add questions and answer options.
4. Set correct answers and scoring.
5. Save and publish.

### 5.7 My Learning (Learners)

1. Go to My Learning.
2. Select an assigned module.
3. Complete blocks in order.
4. Quizzes must be completed to finish training.
5. Certificates appear under My Certificates when available.

## 22. Troubleshooting

### I cannot see a page or menu item

- You do not have access for your role.
- Contact HR or Admin to request access.

### Training module says "Not Found"

- The module is not assigned to your role or user.
- Ask a manager to reassign.

### I cannot submit a form

- Check required fields.
- Try reloading and resubmitting.

### Missing data or incorrect status

- Refresh the page.
- Report to Admin if the issue persists.

## 23. Glossary

- Module: A single training unit.
- Assignment: A training task given to a user or group.
- Path: A sequence of modules.
- SOP: Standard Operating Procedure.
- Role: Access level and permission group.
- Property: Hotel or location.
- Department: A functional team within a property.
- HR Inbox: Central place for approvals and requests.
