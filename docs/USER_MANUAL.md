## 1. Getting Started

### Logging In
1.  Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Safari).
2.  Go to the ALTUS Connect website.
3.  Enter your **Official Hotel Email** and **Password**.
4.  Click **Sign In**.

### Changing Language (English / Arabic)
ALTUS Connect works fully in both languages.
1.  Look for the **Globe Icon** 🌐 in the top-right corner.
2.  Click it to switch between **English** and **العربية**.
3.  The entire screen layout will adjust automatically.

### Updating Your Profile
1.  Click your **Profile Picture** (or initials) in the bottom-left corner.
2.  Select **"My Profile"**.
3.  Here you can:
    *   View your personal information.
    *   Upload a profile picture.
    *   Update your **Wait!** Some details (like Job Title) can only be changed by HR.

## 2. Your Daily Dashboard

When you log in, you see your personal **Dashboard**. This is your "Command Center".

*   **Quick Stats**: See how many tasks or requests you have pending.
*   **My Schedule**: (If applicable) See your upcoming work shifts for the week.
*   **Announcements**: Read important news from the Hotel Management.
*   **Quick Actions**: Large buttons to quickly "Request Leave" or "Report Issue".

## 3. How to Submit Requests

Need a vacation? Need a letter for the bank? You can do it all here.

### Requesting Time Off (Leave)
1.  Click **"My Requests"** in the sidebar menu.
2.  Click the **"+ New Request"** button.
3.  Select **"Leave Request"**.
4.  Fill in the form:
    *   **Leave Type**: Annual, Sick, Unpaid, etc.
    *   **Dates**: Start and End date.
    *   **Reason**: Brief explanation.
5.  Click **Submit**.
    *   *Tip: You will see who needs to approve it (usually your Manager + HR).*

### Requesting Documents (Salary Certificates, etc.)
1.  Go to **"My Requests"** -> **"+ New Request"**.
2.  Select **"Document Request"**.
3.  Choose the type (e.g., "Salary Certificate", "Employment Letter").
4.  Click **Submit**.
    *   *Result: When ready, HR will upload the document here for you to download.*

## 4. Reporting Maintenance Issues

See a broken light or a leaking tap? Don't just ignore it—report it!

1.  Click **"Operations"** -> **"Maintenance"**.
2.  Click **"Submit Ticket"**.
3.  **Location**: Where is the problem? (e.g., "Room 304", "Lobby").
4.  **Description**: What is wrong? (e.g., "AC is making a loud noise").
    *   *Magic Feature: The system will automatically guess the Category (HVAC) and Priority!*
5.  **Photo**: If you are on your mobile, you can take a photo and upload it.
6.  Click **Submit**.

## 5. Training & Knowledge Base

### Knowledge Base (SOPs)
Need to know the hotel's policy on uniforms? Or how to handle a guest complaint?
1.  Click **"Enterprise"** -> **"Knowledge Base"**.
2.  Search for keywords (e.g., "Uniform", "check-in").
3.  Click an article to read it.
    *   *Note: Some important articles require you to click "I Acknowledge" at the bottom.*

### My Training
1.  Click **"My Career"** -> **"Training"**.
2.  You will see courses assigned to you.
3.  Click **"Start"** to watch videos or read content.
4.  Complete the **Quiz** at the end to get your Certificate!

## 6. For Managers: Approvals & Team

If you manage a team, you have extra tools.

### Approving Requests
When a team member requests leave, you get a notification (Email & Bell icon).
1.  Click the notification OR go to **"Requests Inbox"**.
2.  Review the request details.
3.  Click **Approve**, **Reject**, or **Return** (ask for more info).

### Checking Attendance
1.  Go to **"HR & Team"** -> **"My Team"**.
2.  Click **"Shift Schedule"**.
3.  You can see who is working today.
4.  **Correction**: If someone forgot to punch in, you can manually fix their time here.

### Going on Vacations? Delegate!
Don't be a bottleneck while you are away.
1.  Go to **"Requests Inbox"**.
2.  Click **"Delegations"**.
3.  **"Add Delegation"** -> Pick a colleague to handle your approvals while you are gone.
4.  Set the **Start** and **End** dates.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**
A: On the login screen, click "Forgot Password". A reset link will be sent to your email.

**Q: Can I use ALTUS Connect on my phone?**
A: Yes! Open the browser on your phone and go to the same website limit. It works perfectly on mobile.

**Q: Who sees my personal documents?**
A: only You, your Department Head, and the HR Team.

**Q: How do I change my Notification settings?**
A: Click your Profile Picture -> **Settings**. You can turn off emails for less important things if you want.
